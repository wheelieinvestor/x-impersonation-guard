# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being direct, professional, and respectful.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Assuming good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keeping user safety and platform integrity in view.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Encouraging misuse of the tool to silence critics, parody accounts, fan accounts, or unrelated users.
- Sharing credentials, cookies, private browser profiles, or unredacted evidence packages.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
