# Response templates

## "This will silence critics"

That is not the goal and not the design. The tool has parody/fan-account mitigations, a review queue, and explicit docs on what it does not do. It is for impersonation and scam clones, not disagreement.

## "X will ban your reporting account"

Mass reporting can be risky. That is why the default is review-first, dry-run-first, capped, and local. Live submission is a deliberate operator action.

## "This is just automating a manual thing"

Yes. That is the value. The current manual flow is repetitive, slow, and hard to audit. This makes the workflow consistent and reviewable.

## "Why not use ML?"

The first version needs to be explainable. A deterministic score lets users see why something was flagged and why parody/fan accounts were filtered.

## "Can I contribute?"

Yes. Start with issues labeled `good first issue`, run the offline demo, and read `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
